What we train

A well-rounded grappling curriculum; whatever your background, there's a class for you.

Gi

Traditional Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu in the Gi. Build technical fundamentals, grips, sweeps and submissions with a strong focus on body mechanics and leverage.

No-Gi

Fast-paced grappling without the Gi. Develop scrambles, control and submissions in a style that translates directly to MMA and self-defence.

Wrestling

Takedowns, top control and positional dominance. Wrestling fundamentals that sharpen every grappler's game, on the feet and on the mat.

Frequently asked questions

New to the mats? Here's what people usually want to know before their first session.

Do I need experience to start?

No, our Silver membership is built around beginner-friendly fundamentals classes and no prior grappling experience is needed. Everyone starts somewhere.

What should I bring to my first class?

For Gi classes you'll need a Gi; for No-Gi and Wrestling, a rashguard and shorts/spats work well or you can simply wear a tshirt and some tracksuit pants/shorts. Make sure to bring water and stay hydrated!

What age do you train from?

Our Luton classes are for ages 15 and up. We don't currently run a dedicated kids program at this branch.

Can I try a class before committing to a membership?

Yes drop in for £10 a session, no commitment required. First class is free. Message us on Instagram or TikTok to check the best class to start with.

How does membership payment work?

Membership is billed monthly through the MAAT app, no long-term contracts, so you can move between Gold and Silver or pause as needed. Join via MAAT.

Is every class at CYCD?

Almost all with the exception being Tuesday's 7–8:30pm Wrestling class, which runs off-site at Rukana Wrestling Club, 72 New Bedford Rd, Luton LU3 1BT.
